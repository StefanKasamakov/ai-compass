How it works

Three different signals

No single number tells you what is good. Votes show what visitors of this site recommend, installs show what people really run, and stars show what they bookmark.

Find a tool to vote forSuggest a tool

  1. Voting takes one click

    No account and no email. Press the thumbs-up on any tool, here or on its page in Find tools.

  2. One vote per tool

    We count one vote per tool from each network connection, so a single person can't push a tool up the list.

  3. Missing something?

    Suggest it on the Contribute page. A person reviews every suggestion before it appears.
