Watch out

Four rules

  1. Check your school's rules first

    Many schools allow AI for studying but not for writing what you hand in. When in doubt, ask, and say how you used it.

  2. Never trust a citation you did not open

    Chat assistants can invent papers that sound real. Use Consensus, Elicit or Perplexity, and open every source.

  3. Rewording is not your own work

    Paraphrasing tools do not make a text yours. Use them to understand, then write it yourself.

  4. Upload your own material

    NotebookLM answers only from the files you give it, which makes it far less likely to make things up.
