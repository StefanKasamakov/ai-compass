Which one should you use?

Short, honest comparisons of the tools people ask about most. Each one starts with the answer, then shows the prices and what each tool is good at.

ChatGPT vs Claude

Both are excellent everyday assistants and cost the same. ChatGPT does more kinds of things in one app, including images and voice. Claude is the stronger pick for careful writing, long documents and code.

ChatGPT vs Google Gemini

ChatGPT is the better general assistant on its own. Gemini wins if your email, files and calendar already live in Google, because it can read them and it is included in cheaper Google plans.

Claude vs Google Gemini

Claude is the better writer and coder. Gemini is the better helper inside Google's apps and the cheaper way to get a paid plan.

ChatGPT vs Perplexity

These are different tools. ChatGPT is for creating and thinking things through. Perplexity is for looking things up, with links to the sources so you can check them.

ChatGPT vs Microsoft Copilot

Copilot is the convenient choice if you live in Word, Excel and Outlook, because it works inside them with a Microsoft 365 plan. As a standalone assistant, ChatGPT is more capable and more flexible.

Cursor vs GitHub Copilot

Both are for people who write code. GitHub Copilot is the cheaper helper that lives inside the editor you already use. Cursor is a whole editor built around AI and goes further when you want an agent to change many files.

Lovable vs Bolt.new

Both build a working website or app from a description, with no coding. Lovable is the gentler start for a complete beginner. Bolt.new shows more of the code and hosts the result for you, which helps if a developer will take over later.

Midjourney vs Ideogram

Midjourney makes the most artistic, polished pictures but has no free plan. Ideogram is free to start and is the one to use when the picture must contain readable text, such as a poster or logo.

Zapier vs Make

Both connect your apps so routine work happens by itself. Zapier is easier to learn and connects to the most apps. Make is cheaper for the same amount of work and more flexible, but takes longer to learn.

Gamma vs Canva

Gamma is the fastest way from an outline to a finished slide deck. Canva is a full design tool: better when you also need social posts, flyers and brand graphics, and when you want to control the layout yourself.

Suno vs Udio

Both turn a short description into a full song with vocals. Suno is the practical choice today: Udio no longer lets you download songs, so they can only be played on Udio.

Otter.ai vs Fireflies.ai

Both record a meeting, write the transcript and send a summary. Otter is simple and good for one person. Fireflies suits a team that wants to search past meetings and share what was decided.

Runway vs Kling AI

Both make short video clips from text or a picture. Runway is also an editor, so you can remove objects and add sound in the same place. Kling AI focuses on realistic clips and has a free plan to try it.

Grammarly vs QuillBot

Grammarly corrects your writing as you type, in almost every app. QuillBot is for rewording and summarising text you paste in. They solve different problems and many people use both.

HeyGen vs Synthesia

Both make a video of a lifelike presenter reading your script. HeyGen is stronger for marketing and for translating videos with matching lip movement. Synthesia is built for company training and gives more free minutes.
