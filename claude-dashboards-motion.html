Claude news · Explainer · 8 October 2026

Claude Dashboards and Claude Motion, explained.

The short version. Claude can now turn your company's data into a dashboard that keeps itself up to date, and turn a report or chart into a short animated video. Both are in beta. On the same day, Claude Docs, Slides and Design became available to everyone, including the free plan.

Anthropic's 82-second announcement. The video loads from YouTube only when you press play.

Claude Dashboards

Ask a question about your business data in plain words and get a dashboard that updates itself.

What it does
Claude connects to where your company keeps its data, writes the queries and draws the charts. When the data changes, the dashboard follows. Each chart shows when it was last refreshed.
Where the data can come from
Data warehouses such as Amazon Redshift, BigQuery, ClickHouse, Databricks and Snowflake, CRM tools such as Salesforce, and other connectors you have set up in Claude.
How you check the numbers
Click any number to see the query behind it, or ask Claude to explain how it was calculated. Worth doing before a number goes into a board deck.
Sending it elsewhere
A dashboard can be sent on to tools such as Amplitude, Grafana, Hex, Mixpanel, PostHog or Sigma. Looker, monday.com and Tableau are listed as coming soon.
Who gets it
Beta, on all paid plans (Pro, Max, Team, Enterprise). On Enterprise it is off until an admin turns it on under Organization settings, Artifacts.
Try it. In Claude, write: "Build a dashboard of this quarter's revenue by region." Claude asks which data source to use if you have more than one connected.

Claude Motion

Turn a report or a chart into a short animated explainer for a meeting or a presentation.

What it does
Claude builds a short video, for example a 30-second explainer, that animates text, charts, shapes and images. It plays like a video.
Why it is different
It is not a video-generating AI. Claude writes the animation as code, so there is no invented footage and there are no AI-made people, and you can change any word, number or timing afterwards, by editing it or by asking Claude.
What you get
An MP4 file to download, or a project you can open in video tools such as Adobe, Descript, HeyGen, Luma AI or Runway. Canva and Captions are listed as coming soon.
Who gets it
Beta, on Team and Enterprise plans only. Pro and Max do not have it yet.
Try it. Type /motion in the Claude message box, then describe what the video should explain, or attach the report.

Docs, Slides and Design for everyone

The same day, Claude Docs, Claude Slides and Claude Design left beta and opened to every plan, including Free. You can share them outside your organization if your admin allows it, download decks as PowerPoint or PDF, send them to Google Slides as editable files, and edit them in the Claude mobile app. The separate Claude Design website (claude.ai/design) closes on 14 December 2026; Design already works inside every Claude conversation.
