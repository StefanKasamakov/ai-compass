ChatGPT vs Google Gemini

The short answer. ChatGPT is the better general assistant on its own. Gemini wins if your email, files and calendar already live in Google, because it can read them and it is included in cheaper Google plans.

Which one should you pick?

Choose ChatGPT if

  • You want the strongest all-round answers for writing and problem solving
  • You do not use Gmail or Google Docs much
  • You want image creation and voice chat in the same place

Choose Google Gemini if

  • Your mail and documents are in Gmail, Docs and Drive
  • You want a capable assistant at a lower monthly price
  • You often need fresh answers backed by Google Search

The details

ChatGPT

by OpenAI

A chat assistant you type or talk to that writes, explains, answers questions and makes images.

People use it to

  • Draft emails, letters and posts
  • Explain a confusing topic in simple words
  • Summarize a long document you upload

What it costs

Free plan. Go (price varies by country), Plus $20/month, Pro from $100/month.

Good to know

It can state wrong facts confidently, so check anything important. Your chats can be used to train models unless you opt out in settings. The Go plan may show ads.

Google Gemini

by Google

Google's chat assistant that answers questions, writes text and works with Gmail, Docs and Drive.

People use it to

  • Ask questions and get answers with Google Search
  • Summarize emails or files in your Google account
  • Plan a trip or a weekly schedule

What it costs

Free plan. Google AI Plus $4.99/month, Google AI Pro $19.99/month, Google AI Ultra from $99.99/month.

Good to know

Answers can be wrong. Google may keep and review chats to improve its services unless you turn off activity saving, so do not paste sensitive data.
