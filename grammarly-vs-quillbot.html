Grammarly vs QuillBot

The short answer. Grammarly corrects your writing as you type, in almost every app. QuillBot is for rewording and summarising text you paste in. They solve different problems and many people use both.

Which one should you pick?

Choose Grammarly if

  • You want mistakes fixed while you write emails and documents
  • You want help with tone: more polite, more confident, shorter
  • You want it to work everywhere without copying text around

Choose QuillBot if

  • You want to say the same thing in different words
  • You want an article summarised into key points
  • You need citations generated for school work

Grammarly

by Grammarly

Checks spelling, grammar and tone as you type, and can rewrite sentences for you.

People use it to

  • Fix spelling and grammar in emails and documents
  • Make a message sound more polite or confident
  • Rewrite a paragraph to be shorter and clearer

What it costs

Free plan. Pro EUR 12/month (price shown in EU; varies by country).

Good to know

Once installed it reads the text you type in many apps. Check your employer's rules before using it on confidential documents.
