Watch out

Four rules

  1. Customer data is personal data

    Do not paste customers' names, emails or orders into a free chat app. Business plans of most assistants promise not to train on your data; check before you use real records.

  2. Check every fact, price and promise

    AI writes confidently. Read anything that goes to a customer before you send it.

  3. Images need the right licence

    Some image tools only allow commercial use on paid plans. Check the terms before printing a logo or an advert.

  4. Automations act for you

    A Zapier or Make flow keeps running while you sleep. Test it with fake data first and check it weekly.
