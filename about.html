About

Who makes this, and how.

Which AI Map is an independent guide for people who want to use AI without following the industry every day. No company pays to be listed and there are no ads.

How things get on the site

  1. A program checks the sources

    Every few hours it reads the official blogs of the AI companies, GitHub, the Ollama library and a few trusted writers, and collects what is new.

  2. AI writes the plain-English summary

    Each tool and news item gets a short explanation based on its own documentation, never on marketing claims. Summaries can contain mistakes: tell us when you spot one.

  3. A person decides what stays

    Unsafe, empty or misleading projects are removed. Suggestions from visitors are reviewed by a human before they appear.

Privacy

What we store about you

Almost nothing.

Reading the site
No account, no advertising or analytics cookies. Your light or dark theme choice and the tools you voted for are remembered only in your own browser.
Voting
We keep a scrambled, one-way fingerprint of your network address next to the vote, only to stop double voting. It cannot be turned back into your address.
Sending a suggestion
We store what you write. A name and contact are optional; contact details are visible only to reviewers and are never published.
Where it lives
Pages are served by GitHub Pages. Votes and suggestions are stored with Supabase in the EU (Frankfurt). Tool logos and news pictures load from their owners' servers.
