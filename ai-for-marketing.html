Watch out

Four rules

  1. Keep a brand voice file

    Write five sentences about your tone and three things you never say, and paste them at the start of every request (or save them as a Claude project or ChatGPT custom instructions).

  2. Only clone voices and faces with permission

    Voice and avatar tools can copy a real person. Use only your own, or someone who agreed in writing.

  3. Know the commercial rights

    Free plans of music, image and video tools often forbid commercial use or add watermarks.

  4. Say when it matters

    Platforms increasingly label AI-made images and video. Disclose it where people would expect to know.
